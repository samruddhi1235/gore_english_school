About Us

Gore English School, Chousala, also offers its students an opportunity to participate in the Navodaya Vidyalaya scholarship program, which is a prestigious initiative aimed at identifying and nurturing talented students from rural and underprivileged backgrounds. The school actively encourages students to apply for this scholarship, providing them with the guidance and support needed for success in the competitive entrance exams.

Apart from academic excellence, Gore English School, Chousala, places significant emphasis on the overall development of its students through a wide range of extracurricular activities. The school encourages students to participate in various sports, such as cricket, football, and volleyball, ensuring that physical fitness is integrated into their routine. Regular sports events are organized, fostering teamwork, discipline, and a competitive spirit among students. The school also supports indoor activities like chess and table tennis, offering a well-rounded approach to physical education. Through these activities, students are not only able to stay active but also learn important life skills like leadership and perseverance.
