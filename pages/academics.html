Academics

Gore English School, Chousala, was established in 2004 with a vision to provide quality education through the English medium. The school was founded to meet the growing demand for a progressive and modern educational institution in the region. Since its inception, the school has aimed to foster holistic development in students, focusing not just on academics but also on the moral, physical, and social aspects of their growth. The founders, recognizing the importance of a strong educational foundation, ensured that the school followed the Maharashtra State Board curriculum, which is widely recognized and respected.

From its early days, Gore English School prioritized a blend of traditional teaching methods and modern techniques, making learning engaging and effective. The school started with just a few classes, gradually expanding over the years to offer education from 1st to 10th grade. The institution's commitment to excellence in education is reflected in its consistent 100% pass rate for 12 batches of students, a testament to the dedication and hard work of both the students and the teaching staff.
