Classes Offered at Gore English School Chousala

We offer education from Class 1st to 10th with a focus on holistic development and academic excellence.

Class 1st

Class 1st has five divisions: A, B, C, D, and E, with a total of 161 students. Division A has 35, B has 36, C has 33, D has 31, and E has 26 students. Students learn basic English, Mathematics, Marathi, and Environmental Studies. Learning focuses on alphabets, numbers, reading, writing, and basic concepts. Fun activities, drawing, storytelling, and interactive lessons make learning enjoyable.

Class 2nd

Class 2nd has five divisions: A, B, C, D, and E, with a total strength of 186 students. Division A has 37, B has 36, C has 36, D has 38, and E has 39 students. Main subjects include English, Mathematics, Marathi, and Environmental Studies. Students improve reading, writing, addition, subtraction, and sentence formation skills. Activity-based learning helps students understand concepts easily.

Class 3rd

Class 3rd has six divisions: A, B, C, D, E, and F, with a total strength of 234 students. Division A has 41, B has 40, C has 40, D has 40, E has 40, and F has 33 students. Students study English, Mathematics, Marathi, Science, and Social Studies. They develop reading, writing, multiplication, division, and basic scientific knowledge. Topics such as plants, animals, moral values, and society are introduced.

Class 4th

Class 4th has five divisions: A, B, C, D, and E, with a total strength of 218 students. Division A has 44, B has 42, C has 45, D has 42, and E has 45 students. Subjects include English, Mathematics, Marathi, Science, and Social Studies. Students develop problem-solving, creative writing, and scientific thinking skills. They also learn about energy, civilizations, maps, and their surroundings.

Class 5th

Class 5th has four divisions: A, B, C, and D, with a total strength of 135 students. Division A has 34, B has 34, C has 34, and D has 33 students. Students study English, Mathematics, Marathi, Science, and Social Studies. Learning includes grammar, decimals, fractions, geometry, and basic scientific concepts. Students also explore the water cycle, experiments, government, and society.

Class 6th

Class 6th has four divisions: A, B, C, and D, with a total strength of 134 students. Division A has 34, B has 34, C has 33, and D has 33 students. Main subjects include English, Mathematics, Science, Marathi, and Social Science. Students learn algebra, data handling, motion, basic chemistry, and biology. The curriculum encourages logical thinking, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Class 7th

Class 7th has four divisions: A, B, C, and D, with a total strength of 145 students. Division A has 36, B has 38, C has 34, and D has 37 students. Students study English, Mathematics, Science, Marathi, and Social Science. They develop deeper understanding of mathematics, science, languages, and society. Practical activities and projects support their overall academic development.

Class 8th

Class 8th has four divisions: A, B, C, and D, with a total strength of 130 students. Division A has 31, B has 32, C has 34, and D has 33 students. Subjects include English, Mathematics, Science, Marathi, and Social Science. Students strengthen their analytical, scientific, communication, and problem-solving skills. The curriculum prepares students for higher secondary-level studies.

Class 9th

Class 9th has three divisions: A, B, and C, with a total strength of 127 students. Division A has 39, B has 44, and C has 44 students. Students study English, Mathematics, Science, Marathi, and Social Science along with other prescribed subjects. Greater emphasis is given to concepts, practical learning, and examination preparation. Students build a strong academic foundation for their board-level studies.

Class 10th

Class 10th has two divisions: A and B with a total strength of 89 students. Division A has 44 students and Division B has 45 students. Students focus on English, Mathematics, Science, Marathi, and Social Science along with prescribed subjects. Special emphasis is given to revision, practical learning, and examination preparation. Teachers guide students towards strong academic performance and successful board examinations.
