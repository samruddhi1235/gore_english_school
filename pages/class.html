Classes Offered at Gore English School Chousala

We offer education from Class 1st to 10th with a focus on holistic development and academic excellence.

Class 1st

In 1st class, students begin with basic subjects like English, Mathematics, and Environmental Studies, focusing on alphabets, numbers, and storytelling. Learning is activity-based, involving drawing, coloring, and oral lessons.

Class 2nd

IIn 2nd class, they progress to simple sentence formation, basic addition and subtraction, and an introduction to environmental awareness. Rhymes and interactive methods enhance their understanding.

Class 3rd

In 3rd class, students start reading and writing more confidently, learning multiplication and division in Mathematics, and exploring basic science concepts like plants and animals. Moral values and social science topics are also introduced.

Class 4th

In 4th class, students develop creative writing skills in English and solve word problems in Mathematics. Science covers energy sources, while Social Studies introduces early civilizations and map reading.

Class 5th

In 5th class, they work on advanced grammar, decimals, and basic geometry. Science lessons focus on the water cycle and simple experiments, while Social Studies explores government and economic concepts.

Class 6th

In 6th class students delve into essay writing, algebra, and data handling. They study physics concepts like motion, as well as fundamental chemistry and biology topics.

Class 7th

In 7th class, students enhance their literature and grammar skills while solving problems in ratio, proportion, and probability. Science lessons cover ecosystems, force, and energy, while Social Studies focuses on civics and economic activities.

Class 8th

In 8th class, they engage in critical thinking and advanced writing in English. Mathematics introduces geometry, statistics, and linear equations, while Science covers physics (motion, light), chemistry (elements), and biology (cells). Social Studies provides a deeper understanding of Indian history and world geography.

Class 9th

In 9th class marks the beginning of high school-level academics, with advanced literature, algebra, trigonometry, and in-depth science concepts like electricity, chemical reactions, and human anatomy. Social Studies covers the Indian Constitution and world history.

Class 10th

In 10th class, students prepare for the SSC Board Examination with complex topics such as calculus, organic chemistry, genetics, and mechanics. Social Studies includes a detailed study of India’s freedom struggle and world politics. The school has consistently achieved 100% pass results for 12 batches, ensuring a strong academic foundation for students.
