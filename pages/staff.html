Staff

Gore English School, Chousala, has a well-structured and dedicated staff to ensure the smooth functioning of the institution. The school has a total of 72 staff members including 60 teaching staff who are highly qualified and committed to providing quality education. These teachers specialize in various subjects, ensuring that students receive comprehensive academic guidance across all grades. Their dedication has contributed to the school's consistent 100% pass rate for multiple batches, reflecting the institution's strong educational standards.

In addition to the teaching faculty, the school has 8 non-teaching staff, who assist in administrative work, and 4 other support staff, responsible for maintaining cleanliness, security, and overall campus management. Together, these 72 members play a crucial role in creating a well-organized and disciplined learning environment, ensuring that students receive both academic excellence and holistic development.
